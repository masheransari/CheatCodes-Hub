## Rights and quality gate

**Being publicly readable is not equivalent to permission to redistribute an entire data compilation.** Plain factual inputs can often be independently documented, but publishers' unique wording, editorial selection and arrangement, and artwork may carry rights. Attribution helps trace provenance but cannot replace required permission. Game names are used to identify compatibility, not to imply affiliation. This is not legal clearance.

Research entries here use original minimal descriptions and factual input strings collected from published comparison references; **legal reuse and platform behavior have not been independently approved**. If zero possibility of legal objection is required, no commercial release can be certified on that basis.

## GTA VI conflict

- https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-cheats-list/ reports a cheat system and testing around launch.
- https://actuallyop.com/gta6/cheat-codes says none confirmed as of its check.
- https://grindcoast.com/gta-6-news/gta-6-cheat-codes-confirmed claims 19 cross-reported sequences.

These claims cannot be reconciled simply by counting publications. `gta-vi.json` therefore contains no codes. Source-reported codes should be added only after the exact input, edition, platform, unlock condition, and evidence are verified; label such entries `reported_unverified` unless tested.

## Entry verification meaning

- `single_source_not_device_tested`: published in one reference checked in research.
- `cross_referenced_not_device_tested`: information appears in two cited references; no game test performed.
- `tested_on_device`: only after the project records a game session test with platform, edition, build and result.

## Important coverage caveat

The game catalog's `supportedPlatformIds` reflect candidate platforms for future research, **not availability of all code inputs on those platforms**. Render platform filters based on an entry's `inputs[].platformIds`, not on a global assumption that every listed title has working cheats across all systems.
